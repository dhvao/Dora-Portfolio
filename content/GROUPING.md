## Decisions

- The `digitale procreate` folder gives an explicit medium grouping. Its visual subjects justify four smaller series. The line-drawn narrative figures are separate from the green colour-and-body series, and animal studies have their own destination.
- The `crvene rješene` folder is a coherent red drawing group. Two loose root-folder red drawings (22 and 23) join this collection. Interiors and urban exteriors are separated into two related series.
- `kvalitetnije` means a quality selection, not an artistic theme. Its four images are included with the related architectural studio works rather than shown as an unrelated collection.
- Root-folder figure drawings and paintings are grouped into Figure studies; two figures embedded in city grids (20 and 21) sit with City & structure.
- IDs 37/41, 40/42, 46/47, and 63/66 depict the same compositions with small export differences. Primaries are 41, 42, 46, and 63. The other four files are retained as alternate versions on those artwork pages. There were no byte-identical duplicates.
- IDs 5/22 and 8/23 are related drawings/versions with substantial framing or compositional differences, so both remain independently browsable.
- IDs 49–51 differ from the portfolio work: architectural photography/illustrations, including two saved screenshots with website controls and visible third-party signatures. They live on a separate Reference shelf with authorship unconfirmed. They are not included in the 71-work portfolio count. Confirm source attribution and intended use with the artist before changing that classification.
- ID 22 produced Pillow's truncated-file warning. It successfully decoded using tolerant mode and was inspected at larger size; the visible drawing is intact. Its original is preserved unchanged. Replace it if the artist supplies a clean source.

## Accounting

78 supplied files = 71 primary artwork entries + 4 alternate exports + 3 references. Nothing is discarded or generatively altered. Web copies preserve the full image and proportions, normalize EXIF orientation, and flatten transparent pixels onto white. Original photos, including paper edges and saved-image interface elements, remain uncropped.

Originals total about 550 MB (decimal). Two WebP sizes per image total about 44 MiB. The homepage shows a small selection; the full collection is accessible through collection → series → artwork pages or the searchable archive.
